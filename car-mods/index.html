How Accurate Are These Calculators?

🔢

Engineering Formulas

We use established formulas from motorsport engineering — the Wallace Racing ET formula, compressor map principles for turbo sizing, and physics-based 0-60 estimates. These give reasonable ballparks, not guarantees.

📊

Verify with Real Data

Calculated HP gains from mods vary widely by car, tune quality, and supporting modifications. Always verify on a rolling road (dyno) or at a drag strip. Our numbers are a starting point for your build planning.

⚙️

Multiple Variables

Real-world performance depends on traction, launch technique, gearing, ambient temperature, altitude, and fuel quality. Formula-based estimates cannot account for all of these — treat them as targets, not results.
