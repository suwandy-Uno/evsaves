Disclaimer

Important information about how to interpret and use EV Saves calculator results.

Summary: This calculator provides estimates only. Actual fuel costs, electricity tariffs, insurance premiums, servicing costs, taxes, and vehicle prices vary by location, supplier, driver profile, and vehicle. Do not make major financial decisions based solely on these estimates.

Estimates Only

All figures produced by EV Saves calculators are estimates only. They are intended to provide a general indication of potential costs and savings, not precise financial forecasts or guarantees. Results should be treated as a useful starting point for further research — not a definitive answer.

What Affects Your Actual Costs

Actual results will vary based on, but not limited to, the following factors:

  • Your specific vehicle make, model, age, condition, and battery state of health
  • Your personal driving style, speed, and typical routes
  • Your home electricity tariff and energy supplier
  • Local petrol, diesel, or gas pump prices at the time of purchase
  • Your specific insurance provider and personal risk profile (age, postcode/zip, no-claims history)
  • Servicing requirements specific to your vehicle and manufacturer schedule
  • Changes to government policy (VED rates, BIK percentages, stamp duty, tax credits)
  • EV battery degradation over time, which affects range and efficiency
  • Public charging network usage patterns and the tariffs available in your area
  • Ambient temperature and seasonal variation, which significantly affects real-world EV efficiency

UK-Specific Disclaimers

UK Vehicle Excise Duty (VED) rates, Benefit in Kind (BIK) percentages, and EV Chargepoint Grant availability are subject to annual review by HMRC and DVLA. Rates shown in our calculators reflect published figures at the time of last update. Always verify current rates at gov.uk before making decisions.

Salary sacrifice figures are illustrative. The actual tax and National Insurance savings depend on your individual tax code, employer scheme rules, and HMRC guidance. Consult your employer's salary sacrifice provider or a qualified accountant.

Australia-Specific Disclaimers

Stamp duty concessions for EVs in Australia vary significantly by state and territory and are subject to change without notice. The figures shown in our stamp duty calculator are indicative only. Always verify with your relevant State Revenue Office before purchasing a vehicle. Fringe Benefits Tax (FBT) exemption eligibility depends on specific conditions including salary packaging arrangements and employer type — seek advice from a qualified accountant or financial advisor.

US-Specific Disclaimers

The federal EV tax credit under the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) is subject to eligibility conditions including income limits, vehicle MSRP caps, North American assembly requirements, and battery sourcing rules that change over time. Not all vehicles qualify. The IRS maintains the definitive and up-to-date list of eligible vehicles at irs.gov. Our tax credit calculator provides a simplified estimate only. Consult a qualified tax professional to determine your actual credit.

Price Data

Fuel, electricity, and other price data used in our calculators is sourced from publicly available data (RAC Fuel Watch, Ofgem, NRMA, EIA, etc.) and updated periodically. Prices change frequently and the figures displayed may not reflect current market rates in your specific location. Always verify prices with your energy supplier and local fuel stations.

Car Modification Calculators

Performance calculators (horsepower gain, 0-60, quarter mile, turbo sizing, boost pressure) use simplified mathematical formulas and provide rough estimates only. Actual performance depends on many variables including driver skill, vehicle preparation, atmospheric conditions, fuel quality, and component quality. These calculators are for planning and entertainment purposes only. Never make safety-critical decisions based solely on these estimates.
