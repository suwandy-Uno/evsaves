Important information about how to use EV Saves calculators

Summary: This calculator provides estimates only. Actual fuel costs, electricity tariffs, insurance premiums, servicing costs, taxes, and vehicle prices vary by location, supplier, driver profile, and vehicle.

Calculator Estimates

All figures produced by EV Saves calculators are estimates only. They are intended to provide a general indication of potential costs and savings, not precise financial forecasts.

Actual results will vary based on, but not limited to, the following factors:

  • Your specific vehicle make, model, age, and condition
  • Your personal driving style and typical routes
  • Your home electricity tariff and supplier
  • Local petrol and diesel pump prices
  • Your insurance provider and personal risk profile
  • Servicing requirements specific to your vehicle
  • Changes to government policy (road tax, grants, emissions zones)
  • Battery degradation over time
  • Public charging network usage patterns and local tariffs
