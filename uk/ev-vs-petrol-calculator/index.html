How the Calculation Works

Break-Even

Frequently Asked Questions

Most UK drivers save between £800 and £1,500 per year on fuel alone when switching from petrol to electric, depending on mileage, home electricity tariff, and how much public charging they use. Including road tax (£190) and servicing savings (~£200), total annual savings often exceed £1,200.
As of June 2026, the average unit rate under Ofgem's price cap is around 24.5p per kWh. If you're on an EV-specific overnight tariff such as Octopus Go, you can charge for as little as 7–9p per kWh, dramatically improving your savings. This calculator defaults to the standard cap rate.
With a typical EV purchase premium of £6,000–£10,000 and annual running cost savings of £1,200–£1,800, most UK drivers break even within 4–7 years. Higher annual mileage or cheaper overnight electricity tariffs significantly reduce the break-even period. Use the calculator above with your own numbers for a personalised estimate.
EV insurance is currently around £100–£200 per year more expensive than equivalent petrol car cover in the UK, primarily due to higher repair costs for EV-specific components and battery replacement risk. However, this gap is narrowing as insurers gain more data and the market becomes more competitive.
