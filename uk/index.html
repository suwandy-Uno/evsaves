UK EV FAQ

Most UK drivers save £800–£1,500 per year on fuel alone. Including road tax (£190) and servicing savings (£150–£300), total annual savings typically reach £1,100–£2,000 for average mileage drivers.
Under the current Ofgem price cap (June 2026), the average unit rate is 24.5p/kWh. EV-specific overnight tariffs like Octopus Go can cut this to 7–9p/kWh, significantly improving savings.
From April 2025, EVs registered on or after 1 April 2025 pay standard Vehicle Excise Duty (VED). However, older EVs and many current models still benefit from lower rates. The saving versus an average petrol car is around £190/year.
With a typical EV premium of £6,000–£10,000 and annual savings of £1,200–£1,800, most drivers break even in 4–7 years. Higher mileage and cheap overnight charging significantly shorten this. Use our EV vs Petrol Calculator for a personalised figure.
