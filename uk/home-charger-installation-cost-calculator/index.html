Is a Home EV Charger Worth Installing?

For most UK EV drivers who have off-street parking, a home charger is one of the best investments they can make. The upfront cost is typically recovered within one to two years through savings on public charging, and the convenience of waking up to a full charge every morning is significant.

What does a home charger cost in the UK? A standard 7kW smart charger unit costs between £500 and £900 for well-known brands such as Ohme, Zappi (myenergi), Wallbox, and Pod Point. Installation by an approved OZEV-registered electrician typically adds £200–£500 depending on the complexity of your electrical supply and cable routing.

The OZEV EV Chargepoint Grant (previously the EVHS — Electric Vehicle Homecharge Scheme) provides up to £350 off the purchase and installation of a smart home charger. To be eligible, you must: own, lease or have ordered a qualifying EV or plug-in hybrid; have private off-street parking; and use an OZEV-approved installer. Renters in houses can also apply, but flat renters must apply under the separate EVHS for flat owners/renters scheme. Always verify current eligibility at gov.uk as terms change.

Smart charging features matter for maximising savings. Smart chargers (required to qualify for the grant) can schedule charging during off-peak times. On tariffs like Octopus Go or Intelligent Octopus, this means charging for 7–9p per kWh overnight instead of the standard 24.5p cap rate — a saving of up to 65% on your charging costs. The Zappi charger also supports solar self-consumption, maximising the use of your own generation before drawing from the grid.
