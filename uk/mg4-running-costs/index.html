MG4 Running Costs UK: Why It's the Best-Value EV for Many Buyers

The MG4 has been a genuine phenomenon in the UK EV market. Since its launch in late 2022, it has consistently outsold premium rivals from established European brands while offering a running cost profile that is difficult to match at any price point. For buyers prioritising the lowest total cost of ownership, the MG4 deserves serious consideration — and the numbers here will show you exactly why.

MG4 Specifications and Battery Options

The MG4 is offered in the UK in several battery configurations. The Standard Range variant uses a 51 kWh battery (64 kWh is the Long Range). The most popular variant for buyers seeking the best balance of range and price is the MG4 Long Range, featuring a 64 kWh usable battery and 203 bhp rear-wheel drive motor. In real-world UK driving, the Long Range achieves approximately 3.8 miles per kWh in mixed conditions — excellent for a family hatchback of its class. WLTP range is quoted at 281 miles; genuine real-world range in temperate UK conditions is typically 220–255 miles.

MG also offers an XPOWER all-wheel drive variant with dual motors and 429 bhp for those who prioritise performance over efficiency. The XPOWER's real-world efficiency drops to approximately 3.2 mi/kWh, so the Long Range single motor remains the recommended choice for cost-focused buyers.

Electricity Costs for the MG4

At 3.8 mi/kWh across 10,000 annual miles, the MG4 Long Range consumes approximately 2,632 kWh per year. On Octopus Go at 7p/kWh, annual electricity costs are approximately £184. At the standard Ofgem tariff of 24.5p/kWh, the same mileage costs £645. The MG4 supports DC fast charging at up to 135 kW, enabling a 10–80% charge in around 35 minutes at a compatible rapid charger. For regular public charging at 75p/kWh, a typical top-up from 20–80% costs approximately £24.

One practical advantage: the MG4's battery management system supports vehicle-to-load (V2L) charging on some variants, allowing the car's battery to power external devices — useful for camping, outdoor events, or powering tools on a worksite.

The 7-Year Warranty Advantage

MG offers a 7-year/80,000-mile vehicle warranty on the MG4, which is the longest factory warranty of any mainstream car brand in the UK. This compares to 4 years from Tesla, 3–5 years from most European brands, and 6 years from BYD. The 7-year warranty substantially reduces the financial uncertainty of ownership over a longer period — the primary risk with any new EV brand is battery or powertrain failure, and MG's warranty eliminates this as a meaningful cost risk for the first seven years. MG also provides an 80,000-mile battery warranty guaranteeing at least 70% capacity retention.

MG4 Insurance Costs in the UK

This is one area where the MG4 genuinely impresses. Insurance groups for the MG4 typically run from 21 to 31 depending on variant — considerably lower than BYD, Tesla, or Volkswagen Group equivalents. Annual premiums for a typical experienced driver are approximately £750–£1,000, making the MG4 one of the cheapest EVs to insure in the UK. MG has an established UK presence dating back decades (now owned by SAIC Motor), with a well-developed approved repairer and parts network — a key factor in keeping insurance costs down.

Servicing Costs

MG4 servicing is competitively priced through MG's UK dealer network, with first and second year services typically costing £100–£150. Longer-term annual servicing averages £130–£180. The rear-wheel drive powertrain is simple and well-proven across MG's global markets. Brake wear is minimal thanks to regenerative braking. Tyre costs are the primary variable expense — budget £120–£200 per year for tyre replacement over a typical ownership period. Total annual servicing costs typically run £150–£200.

MG4 vs Volkswagen ID.3: The Value Comparison

The Volkswagen ID.3 is the MG4's most direct European rival — similar size, similar battery capacity, similar target market. The ID.3 58 kWh Pro lists at approximately £36,000–£38,000 in the UK. The MG4 Long Range lists at approximately £28,000–£30,000. That £8,000 price difference buys a similar real-world experience: both achieve 3.7–4.0 mi/kWh in practice, both have comparable charging speeds (ID.3 supports up to 130 kW DC), and both target family hatchback buyers. The ID.3 offers marginally stronger residual values and a more established service network, but the MG4's price advantage, 7-year warranty, and lower insurance costs make it the better value proposition for most private buyers calculating total cost of ownership over a typical 3–5 year period.

SAIC Ownership and Brand Context

MG is owned by SAIC Motor, one of China's largest automotive manufacturers. This sometimes raises questions about brand longevity and parts supply, but SAIC has operated the MG brand in the UK continuously since 2011 and has significant investment in its UK sales and service infrastructure. Parts are readily available through MG's UK dealer network, and the brand's growing market share provides confidence about continued UK presence.

Total Annual Running Cost Summary

For a typical MG4 Long Range owner covering 10,000 miles per year with home overnight charging: electricity approximately £184, servicing £150, insurance £850, road tax £195 (MG4 is under the £40,000 luxury threshold). Total annual running costs excluding depreciation and finance: approximately £1,380 — making the MG4 one of the most affordable EVs to run in the UK market.
