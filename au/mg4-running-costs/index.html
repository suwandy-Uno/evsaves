MG4 Running Costs: Australia's Best-Value EV?

The MG4 has become one of the most talked-about EVs in Australia, and for good reason. With prices starting from around $38,990 driveaway for the Standard Range and $44,990 for the Long Range, it undercuts most rivals significantly while delivering a competitive 350–452km WLTP range and surprisingly refined driving dynamics.

On the road, the MG4 achieves approximately 5.8–6.2km/kWh in real-world Australian conditions — slightly better than the official WLTP figure suggests in moderate climates. In hotter states like Queensland or Western Australia, efficiency can dip in peak summer months as the cabin air conditioning draws more power. In cooler Victoria or Tasmania, efficiency holds up well year-round.

Charging costs are where the MG4 delivers compelling value. At a national average of 32c/kWh for home electricity, a full charge from 10% to 80% costs around $13–15, providing roughly 240–280km of range. That works out to approximately 5–6c per km — compared to 14–18c per km for a petrol car at $1.89/L doing 10L/100km.

Servicing follows MG's standard schedule: annual or 10,000km intervals (whichever comes first), focused on cabin filter, brake fluid, tyre rotation and software updates. Expect $200–350 per service — significantly cheaper than an equivalent petrol SUV. The 7-year/unlimited-km battery warranty provides strong peace of mind.

Insurance for the MG4 is lower than for Tesla or BYD products, partly due to MG's established repair network in Australia and lower parts costs. Comprehensive policies typically range from $900–$1,400 per year depending on age, postcode and driving history.

Frequently Asked Questions

A full charge (10–100%) on the 64kWh Standard Range battery costs approximately $17–21 at the national average of 32c/kWh. Many states have cheaper off-peak rates — in Victoria, night-time rates can be as low as 18–22c/kWh, reducing a full charge to $12–14.
The MG4 is eligible for the Federal FBT exemption under novated lease arrangements. Several state incentive schemes may also apply — NSW, VIC, QLD and ACT have offered various stamp duty exemptions or rebates. Check your state government's website for current incentives as schemes change regularly.
Real-world range varies from 310–380km for the Standard Range and 400–450km for the Long Range in moderate Australian conditions. Hot weather (above 35°C) with air conditioning running reduces range by 15–20%. Highway driving at 110km/h reduces efficiency compared to city driving.
MG4 servicing costs $200–350 per annual service at an MG dealer. The service intervals are annual or 10,000km (whichever comes first). Major services (at 30,000km and 60,000km) may cost slightly more. There are no oil changes, spark plugs, or exhaust maintenance costs — saving $200–400 per year versus a petrol equivalent.
The MG4 and BYD Atto 3 have very similar annual running costs. The MG4's slightly better efficiency (5.8 vs 5.3km/kWh) gives it a small edge on charging costs, while insurance tends to be comparable. The BYD Atto 3 has a larger battery (60.5kWh vs 64kWh Standard Range) offering similar range. Choose based on size (hatch vs SUV) and price point.
