Frequently Asked Questions

Most UK drivers save between £800 and £1,500 per year on fuel alone when switching from petrol to electric, depending on mileage, home electricity tariff, and how much public charging they use. With road tax and servicing savings included, total annual savings often exceed £1,200.
As of mid-2026, the average unit rate under Ofgem's price cap is around 24.5p per kWh. If you're on an EV-specific overnight tariff (like Octopus Go), you can charge for as little as 7–9p per kWh, dramatically improving your savings.
With a typical EV purchase premium of £6,000–£10,000 and annual running cost savings of £1,200–£1,800, most drivers break even within 4–7 years. Higher mileage drivers, or those on cheap overnight tariffs, often break even faster. Use our calculator to get a personalised estimate.
EV insurance is currently around £100–£200 per year more expensive than equivalent petrol car cover in the UK, due to higher repair costs and battery replacement risk. However, this gap is narrowing as insurers gain more EV claims data and competition increases.
