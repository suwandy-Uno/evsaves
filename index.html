Frequently Asked Questions

Savings vary widely by region, driving habits and vehicle choice, but in the UK most drivers save £800–£1,500 per year on fuel alone after switching to home charging. US drivers typically save $1,000–$1,800 per year on gas, and Australian drivers save $600–$1,200. Use our country-specific calculators to get a personalised figure based on your actual mileage and local prices.

We update our default prices regularly — typically monthly — drawing from official sources including Ofgem (UK electricity), RAC Fuel Watch (UK petrol), NRMA (Australian fuel), and the US Energy Information Administration (EIA). Each calculator page shows a "last updated" date so you always know how fresh the data is. You can also enter your own prices for a more precise result.

Yes. Our EV vs petrol/gas calculators let you set a home charging percentage and a public/rapid charging rate separately, so you get an accurate blended cost if you can't always charge at home. Public charging costs can be 2–3× higher than home rates, which makes a significant difference to the savings calculation.

The break-even point — the number of years until total EV ownership costs fall below an equivalent petrol or gas car — depends on the price premium of the EV, your annual mileage and local fuel prices. Our Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) calculators compute this for you. In the UK, break-even is typically 3–6 years for a new EV bought outright at 2026 prices.
