Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

This privacy policy explains how EV Saves ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use evsaves.com ("the Site"). We operate calculators for users in the UK, Australia and the United States.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you use our contact form, we collect the name, email address, subject and message content you submit. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry and is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Calculator inputs

All calculator inputs (mileage, tariff rates, vehicle data, etc.) are processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. We do not transmit, store, or log any calculator inputs on our servers. Your calculation data stays on your device.

Automatically collected data

Like most websites, we collect standard web server log data including IP addresses, browser type, referring URLs, and pages visited. This data is used to maintain site security and improve performance. We do not link this data to individual users.

2. Cookies and Tracking

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic site functionality. No consent is required for these.
  • Analytics cookies: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate site usage — pages visited, time on site, and general geographic region. Data is anonymised and subject to Google's privacy policy. You can opt out via the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Advertising cookies: We display Google AdSense ads. Google may use cookies to serve relevant advertisements based on your browsing behaviour. You can opt out via Google Ads Settings.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — website analytics (Google LLC, USA)
  • Google AdSense — display advertising (Google LLC, USA)
  • Cloudflare Pages — website hosting and CDN (Cloudflare, Inc., USA)

Affiliate links on this site (such as to comparethemarket.com, iselect.com.au, or zebra.com) may use tracking cookies governed by those companies' respective privacy policies. These links are clearly disclosed near the relevant calls to action.

4. International Data Transfers

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and analytics provider (Google) are US-based companies. If you are in the UK or EU, your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

5. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for a maximum of 12 months. Server logs are retained for up to 90 days. Analytics data is retained for 14 months in Google Analytics. We do not sell personal data.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • UK/EU (UK GDPR / GDPR): Right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. Right to lodge a complaint with the ICO (uk.ico.org.uk).
  • Australia (Privacy Act 1988): Right to access and correct personal information we hold. Right to complain to the OAIC (oaic.gov.au).
  • US (varies by state): Certain US state laws (e.g. CCPA for California residents) provide rights including the right to know, delete, and opt-out of sale of personal data. We do not sell personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via our contact page.

7. Children's Privacy

EV Saves is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please use our contact form and select "General question" as the subject, or describe your privacy concern in the message.
