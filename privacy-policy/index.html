Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

This privacy policy explains how EV Saves ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use evsaves.com ("the Site").

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you use our contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message content you submit. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry.

Calculator inputs

All calculator inputs (mileage, tariff, vehicle data) are processed entirely in your browser. We do not transmit, store, or log any calculator inputs on our servers.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites, we collect standard server log data including IP addresses, browser type, referring URLs, and pages visited. This data is used to maintain site security and improve performance. We do not link this data to individual users.

2. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic site functionality. No consent required.
  • Analytics cookies: We may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage. This data is anonymised and subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Advertising cookies: If we display Google AdSense ads, Google may place cookies to serve relevant advertisements. You can opt out via Google's ad settings.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — website analytics
  • Google AdSense — display advertising
  • Cloudflare — hosting and security (Cloudflare Pages)

Affiliate links on this site (such as to comparethemarket.com) may use tracking cookies. These are clearly disclosed near the relevant links.

4. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for a maximum of 12 months. Server logs are retained for up to 90 days. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via our contact page.

6. Children's Privacy

EV Saves is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please use our contact form.
