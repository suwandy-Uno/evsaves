About EV Saves

Real EV Running Costs. Real Savings.

Our Mission

EV Saves exists to cut through the marketing noise around electric vehicles and give UK drivers honest, data-driven answers to one simple question: will switching to an EV actually save me money?

The answer depends on your mileage, your car, your electricity tariff, and a dozen other factors. Generic headlines and manufacturer claims don't help you. A calculator built around your actual numbers does.

What We Do

We build free, accurate running cost calculators for UK EV drivers and prospective buyers. Every calculator uses real UK prices — current petrol pump averages, Ofgem unit rates, and verified vehicle efficiency figures — updated regularly.

We show our working. Every calculation is transparent: you can see the formula, adjust every input, and understand exactly where each figure comes from.

Our Data Sources

  • Petrol prices: RAC Fuel Watch and BEIS weekly fuel price data
  • Electricity prices: Ofgem Energy Price Cap (updated quarterly)
  • Vehicle efficiency: WLTP figures and real-world data from Zap-Map and EV Database
  • Road tax: DVLA Vehicle Excise Duty rates (gov.uk)
  • Insurance: Comparison site averages and ABI data
  • Servicing costs: RAC and AA servicing cost surveys

Accuracy & Limitations

Our calculators provide estimates. Your actual costs will vary based on your specific vehicle, driving style, home energy tariff, local fuel prices, and individual insurance profile.

We update prices regularly but cannot guarantee real-time accuracy. Always verify current prices with your energy supplier and local fuel stations before making financial decisions.
